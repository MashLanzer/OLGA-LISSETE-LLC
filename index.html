Good to know

Questions we get before the walkthrough.

Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. We carry general liability and workers' compensation, and we're happy to provide a certificate of insurance naming your company before service begins.
Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
We bring all cleaning chemicals, machines and consumables. If you'd prefer we restock your restroom and break-room supplies, we can fold that into the monthly contract.
How do you handle after-hours access and security?
We work with your access system — key, fob, code or on-site check-in. The assigned crew is consistent, background-considered, and follows your open/close and alarm procedures.
Is there a long-term contract or auto-renewal?
No surprise auto-renewals. We agree on a term and scope in writing; if something isn't working, you tell us and we fix it. Retention comes from the work, not the paperwork.
What if something gets missed?
Flag it and we return to correct it at no charge. Everything on your scope sheet is our responsibility — that's the whole point of putting it in writing.
